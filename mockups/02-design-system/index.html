Please confirm at Gate 1

  1. Kids body text at 18px (others 16px). OK, or keep every track at 16px?
  2. Projector is a view option, not a fourth track. It sits on top of whichever track is active (the teacher view in milestone 12 and any big-screen use). OK?
  3. New tokens proposed beyond milestone 00: --info, --warn, --danger (callouts), --focus (keyboard focus ring), --border, --btn-shadow. Colours were chosen to pass AA; the numbers shown are measured.
  4. Footer links grow from 40px to 44px tall to meet the 44px minimum. Buttons and menu items stay at 48px.
  5. Callouts always carry an icon and a word (Tip, Careful, Safety), never colour alone.
  6. The Notice banner ("Educational, not for design") is yellow on Kids and Students, and a dark panel with a yellow outline on Pros. OK?
